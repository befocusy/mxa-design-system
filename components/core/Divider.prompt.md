**Divider** — hairline and accent rules.

```jsx
<Divider />                          {/* sand hairline */}
<Divider tone="accent" mark />       {/* short taupe underline under a section label */}
<Divider orientation="vertical" />
```

`tone`: `hairline | accent | strong`. `mark` makes the 48px taupe signature rule MXA places under uppercase section labels.
