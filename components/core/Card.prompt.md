**Card** — container for grouped content: service stages, fee concepts, summary panels. Crisp corners, hairline border, quiet shadow only when raised.

```jsx
<Card variant="flat" accentTop eyebrow="Etapa 1" title="Diagnóstico de la situación actual">
  Análisis de las últimas tres Declaraciones Juradas y de las fuentes de ingresos.
</Card>
<Card variant="ink" title="Entregable">Presentación final en formato PowerPoint.</Card>
```

Variants: `flat | raised | sunken | ink`. Optional `eyebrow` (taupe caps), `title` (Ubuntu Bold), `accentTop` (taupe top rule), `hover` (for clickable). Keep cards square-edged and restrained — no colored left-borders.
