**Badge** — small pill for statuses, document states and metadata.

```jsx
<Badge variant="success" dot>Vigente</Badge>
<Badge variant="accent">Por única vez</Badge>
<Badge variant="neutral">Anexo</Badge>
```

Variants: `neutral | ink | accent | success | danger | blue`. Add `dot` for a status dot. Keep labels short; uppercase optional via your own styling.
